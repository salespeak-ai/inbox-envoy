# Optional personal email signature (D1)

Use only if you actually screen cold email with your agent. Copy the line into your email app's signature settings manually. Inbox Envoy never edits or inserts a signature and does not enable screening.

**Primary, unbranded:**

> Cold emails to me are screened by my AI agent. Here's how it works: https://salespeak.ai/agent-screened-email/

**Policy-style alternative, unbranded:**

> Cold email to this address is screened by my AI agent. What it checks: https://salespeak.ai/agent-screened-email/

No per-user tracking codes. The link is an educational page, not a personal policy disclosure or a promise that specific screening criteria will be revealed. No Salespeak branding is appended to a person's signature.
